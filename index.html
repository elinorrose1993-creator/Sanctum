Why gambling, specifically

One wound, many shapes.

Gambling harm isn't one problem. It's debt and shame, relapse and secrecy, families fractured, young people pulled in by apps built to hook them. Generalist therapy often misses the specifics. Sanctum brings together people who work in exactly this — so you're understood from the first session, not explaining the basics.

A safe space, by design

Anonymous browsing, verified practitioners, and crisis support one tap away — always. Your privacy is the foundation, not an afterthought.
